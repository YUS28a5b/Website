Plans for the Future

Building an Ultimate Hub

The primary goal of this website—and the Audiology page in particular—is to serve as the ultimate hub for audiology.

I firmly believe that the best clinician is someone who can drive the best outcomes with the fewest visits. My aim is to become a clinician whom a patient only needs to see once for a single issue. However, I know this is not always practical because patients may present with more than one issue, and a care plan may require several visits.

The biggest factor that can reduce this number of visits and appointment time is a foundational understanding of the condition and its pathophysiology. That is where this webpage will contribute. It will serve as a great resource for patients. I will try to build each post and page with solid evidence and references to ensure accuracy and reliability.

Currently, my page lacks a lot of things. I mean a lot. So… let’s see where I can improve. If you have any suggestions, feel free to contact me.
