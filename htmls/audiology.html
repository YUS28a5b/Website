Services

Comprehensive & Transparent Care

Every individual’s auditory path is unique. We go beyond basic testing to provide thorough diagnostic evaluations and personalized solutions. Our process is built on clarity and education, ensuring you feel completely confident in your hearing care plan.

  • Comprehensive Hearing Evaluations In-depth air, bone, and speech audiometry to determine the type and degree of your hearing loss.
  • Standard Middle Ear Testing (Tymp and Acoustic Reflex) Routine tympanometry to evaluate eardrum integrity and general middle ear function.
  • Advanced Specialized Testing (If Clinically Indicated) Extended-high frequency audiometry and Patulous Eustachian Tube Dysfunction evaluations for complex symptoms. *Recommended based on individual clinical needs; additional evaluation fees may apply.
  • Evidence-Based Hearing Aid Fitting Objective, real-ear verified hearing aid programming tailored to individual's unique acoustic needs and lifestyle.

Collaborative Diagnostic Partnership

We partner with healthcare professionals to deliver precise, objective audiological diagnostics. Through timely communication and thorough clinical reporting, we complement your practice to ensure comprehensive patient care.

  • Diagnostic Audiology & Tympanometry Thorough baseline evaluations using standardized clinical protocols for accurate diagnosis.
  • Advanced Specialized Testing Offering extended high-frequency audiometry and Patulous Eustachian Tube Dysfunction testing for complex diagnostic cases.
  • Detailed and Updated Technology Consultations Transparent, clinically driven hearing aid recommendations focusing purely on patient efficacy.
  • Collaborative Care with Referring Providers Clear, actionable diagnostic findings delivered promptly to support your medical management.
